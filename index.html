      Who TF is T.M.2.K?

      A St. Louis DJ who builds sets for the people in the front row: his friends.

      Cam Carver plays heavy. His mixes move from bass house intros into dubstep flips, riddim and hip-hop edits, with the doubles and blends that make a crowd lose it. The For My Feral Friends series started as mixes made for his own crew, and the name stuck.

      What started as a hobby turned into nights at Red Flag, Mississippi Underground and The Crack Fox. For Cam, it's about the community as much as the music: the crews, the promoters and the people who show up. His latest mix came out of a livestream, reworked with his own edits in Ableton.
